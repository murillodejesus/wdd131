Project Plan: Nômade Bikeshop

Site Name

Nômade Bikeshop

The name "Nômade" represents the freedom and adventure associated with cycling, evoking the idea of exploring new paths. It's a simple, memorable name that connects directly to the cyclist's lifestyle.

Optional Domain Availability: nomadebikeshop.com.br

Site Purpose

The "Nômade Bikeshop" website's main purpose is to serve as the store's online calling card. It will present the brand and provide essential information about the products and services offered. The platform will serve to:

  • Establish brand identity: Create a professional and attractive online presence.
  • Present products and services: Detail the variety of bikes, parts, accessories, and maintenance services.
  • Generate leads and sales: Attract new customers and encourage them to visit the physical store.
  • Provide contact information: Make it easy to contact the store and provide the address and business hours.

Scenarios

Below are some scenarios a site visitor might have, serving as a guide for the content:

  • "Where is Nômade Bikeshop located and what are its business hours?"
  • "What types of bikes does the shop sell? I'm interested in a mountain bike for beginners."
  • "Does Nômade offer maintenance services? I need an adjustment on my bike's brakes."

Color Schema

The Nômade color palette evokes a sense of adventure, nature, and modernity while maintaining readability. The colors will be applied as follows:

  • Main Background: White (#FFFFFF) or Light Gray (#F5F5F5).
  • Text and Headings: Dark Gray (#1e1e24).
  • Accents and Buttons: Yellow (#fbaf00).
  • Secondary Details: Medium Gray (#888888).

Typography

The typography will be modern and easy to read, using the **Poppins** font from Google Fonts.

  • Headings (h1, h2): Poppins, bold.
  • Body Text (p, li): Poppins, with a regular weight to ensure comfortable reading.
